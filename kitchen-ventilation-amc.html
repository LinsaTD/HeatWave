Annual Maintenance for Kitchen Ventilation Systems in the UAE

Heat Wave Br. Of New Life — Keeping Commercial Kitchens Safe, Clean & Efficient.

Heat Wave provides professional Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for kitchen ventilation systems in restaurants, hotels, commercial kitchens, and catering facilities across the UAE.
