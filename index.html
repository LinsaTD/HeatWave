Our Expertise

Complete fire protection. Every step of the way.

At Heat Wave Br. of New Life Electromechanical Contracting LLC, our expertise lies in delivering complete, integrated fire protection solutions, covering every stage of a project from design and material supply to installation, testing, commissioning, rectification, and ongoing maintenance.

With over 15 years of industry experience in the UAE through our parent company, New Life Electromechanical Contracting LLC, Abu Dhabi, we combine technical knowledge, skilled professionals, and a commitment to quality and safety to meet the diverse requirements of commercial, residential, and industrial facilities.
