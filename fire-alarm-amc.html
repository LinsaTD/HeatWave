Annual Maintenance for Fire Alarm, Firefighting & Emergency Lighting Systems in the UAE

Heat Wave Br. Of New Life — Protecting Lives Through Reliable Fire Safety Maintenance.

Heat Wave provides comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for fire protection and life safety systems across the UAE. Our services help maintain system reliability, emergency readiness, and compliance with applicable Civil Defence requirements.

We support commercial, residential, industrial, and institutional facilities through scheduled inspections, functional testing, preventive maintenance, and fault rectification.

Our AMC Services Include

Fire Alarm Systems: Control panel testing (addressable and conventional), smoke and heat detector cleaning, and manual call point, sounder, and strobe testing.

Firefighting Systems: Inspection and maintenance of electric, diesel, and jockey pumps, sprinkler heads, valves, flow switches, fire hose reels, cabinets, and pressure gauges.

Emergency Lighting Systems: Testing of exit lights, bulkhead lights, self-contained and central battery systems, inverters, and control panels.

Our trained technical team is committed to dependable maintenance, prompt fault rectification, and keeping critical life safety systems ready when needed.
