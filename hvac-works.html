HVAC services

Complete HVAC solutions

Heat Wave provides comprehensive HVAC solutions for new projects, fit-outs, and existing facilities. We specialize in equipment supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance, ensuring reliable performance, energy efficiency, and indoor comfort.

Our Core Services

HVAC Equipment Supply: Supply of air conditioning units, ventilation equipment, and associated HVAC components to meet project specifications.

HVAC System Installation: Professional installation of air conditioning, ventilation, and related mechanical systems for new projects and fit-out works.

Testing & Commissioning: System inspection, performance testing, operational verification, and commissioning to ensure systems function as intended.

Preventive Maintenance: Scheduled inspections, servicing, cleaning, and maintenance to improve system efficiency and minimize unexpected breakdowns.

Corrective Maintenance & Repairs: Troubleshooting, fault diagnosis, repair, and rectification to restore reliable HVAC system operation.

Project Coordination & Handover: Coordination with clients, consultants, and other contractors, along with system documentation, final inspections, and project handover.

We are committed to delivering quality workmanship, cost-effective solutions, and dependable HVAC services tailored to our clients’ needs.
