Fire Stop Sealant Services

Certified Fire Stopping Solutions in Abu Dhabi & UAE

Heat Wave specializes in fire stop sealant solutions designed to prevent the spread of fire, smoke, and toxic gases through joints, gaps, and service penetrations. Our services support passive fire protection in commercial, residential, hospital, hotel, and high-rise projects.

Using tested materials and appropriate installation methods, we aim to deliver reliable fire stopping solutions in accordance with project specifications and applicable Civil Defence requirements.
