About Heat Wave

Trusted fire protection specialists in the UAE

Heat Wave Br. of New Life Electromechanical Contracting LLC is a comprehensive fire protection solutions provider based in Dubai, UAE, specializing in the supply, installation, design, testing, commissioning, and maintenance of fire protection systems.

As the Dubai branch of New Life Electromechanical Contracting LLC, Abu Dhabi, we bring over 15 years of experience in the UAE market, delivering dependable, high-quality, and cost-effective solutions for commercial, residential, and industrial projects.

Our core expertise includes fire protection system projects, fire alarm and firefighting systems, system rectifications, preventive and corrective maintenance, and fit-out works. We provide end-to-end solutions, from system design and material supply to installation, commissioning, troubleshooting, rectification, and ongoing maintenance.

With a strong commitment to quality, safety, regulatory compliance, and customer satisfaction, our professional team strives to ensure that every project meets the required standards and client expectations.

With our expanding presence in Dubai and the establishment of New Life Fire Protection Ltd in London, United Kingdom, we are strengthening our capabilities and working towards our long-term ambition of delivering reliable fire protection and related building services across international markets.
