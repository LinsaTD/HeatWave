Annual Maintenance & Inspection for Fire Stop Sealant Systems in the UAE

Heat Wave Br. Of New Life — Maintaining Fire Barriers. Protecting Lives.

Heat Wave provides professional Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for fire stopping systems in commercial, residential, and industrial facilities across the UAE. Our services help identify damaged, deteriorated, or disturbed fire seals and maintain the integrity of passive fire protection systems.

Our team carries out inspections, necessary rectification, and reapplication using compatible, approved materials in accordance with system specifications and applicable Civil Defence requirements.

Our Fire Stop Sealant AMC Services

Visual Inspection of Fire-Rated Zones

Reapplication of Approved Fire Stop Sealants

Inspection of Pipe & Cable Penetrations

Wall & Floor Joint Condition Checks

Detailed Inspection Reports & Documentation

Customized Maintenance Schedules

Support for ADCD / DCD Inspection Requirements

Heat Wave is committed to maintaining effective fire stopping systems that help restrict the spread of fire and smoke, protect building occupants, and support ongoing safety compliance.
